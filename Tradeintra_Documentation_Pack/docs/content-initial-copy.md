- **Ready-made, not blank-canvas:** a polished Deriv trading template with a brand kit and configurable modules.

- **One operator dashboard:** sites, domains, publish status, and earnings.
- **Clear boundaries:** no custody, no trade execution, no profit promises.
- **Mock-first development:** live Deriv integration only after approval.

## How it works

1. Create an account.
2. Connect Deriv (mock during development; live after approval).
3. Choose the Deriv trading template.
4. Add your logo, colours, copy, and risk disclosure.
5. Choose a free subdomain or buy/connect a domain.
6. Preview and publish.
7. Manage publish status, domains, and earnings.
