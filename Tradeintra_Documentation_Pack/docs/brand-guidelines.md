# Tradeintra Brand Guidelines

## Brand basics
- Wordmark: `tradeintra` (lowercase preferred)
- Tagline: `Build. Deploy. Trade.`
- Product: a platform for creating, customizing, managing, and deploying trading platforms
- Personality: modern, confident, technically capable, clear, professional

## Official colors
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `brand-yellow` | `#FEDD00` | CTAs, key highlights, active states, small logo accent |
| `brand-purple` | `#0D000A` | Primary brand color, dark surfaces, headings, navigation |
| `brand-white` | `#F3F2F2` | Light backgrounds, cards, light surfaces, text on dark |

Supporting tokens may include carefully selected neutral shades for borders, secondary text, disabled states, and subtle surface differentiation. Keep them subordinate to the three agreed colors.

## Logo direction
Explore a custom lowercase `t` symbol:
- Primarily deep purple.
- A restrained yellow accent.
- Strong silhouette at small sizes.
- Legible on light and dark surfaces.
- Works as a standalone favicon/app icon.
- Avoid generic chart arrows, coins, globes, or stock symbols unless a strong original design rationale exists.
- Avoid relying on gradients, shadows, or animation to make the logo recognizable.

Required variants:
1. Horizontal wordmark + symbol.
2. Light-background version.
3. Dark-background version.
4. Symbol-only.
5. Favicon.
6. App icon.

Use SVG for scalable logo assets where possible. Confirm final logo approval with the owner before broad implementation.

## Typography
Evaluate Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Manrope, and Poppins. Choose one primary family based on readability, available weights, licensing, and visual fit. Use a restrained type scale and avoid mixing too many families.

## UI principles
- Clean, spacious layouts.
- Clear hierarchy and concise labels.
- Consistent spacing and alignment.
- Subtle borders and shadows.
- Strong but restrained yellow CTAs.
- Purple for structure and contrast.
- Neutral white for breathing room.
- Consistent icon style.
- Charts must use real data or be explicitly labeled as demo data.
- Customer sites use a ready-made template with a constrained brand kit; do not expose a free-form page builder.
- Required risk disclosures must remain visible and legible.
- Do not copy DForge’s visual identity, copy, or claims. Use the reference for product behavior, not for brand imitation.

## Design differentiation
Tradeintra should feel like a credible infrastructure product, not a generic affiliate template:
- use the yellow/purple/white system with restraint;
- prioritize status, domain, and publish clarity;
- use real screenshots/previews of the product, not fabricated dashboards;
- label simulated Deriv data visibly;
- avoid casino/trading-hype imagery and profit claims.

## Buttons
- Primary: yellow fill, deep-purple text if contrast is adequate; verify contrast. If the chosen pairing fails the contrast target, use deep-purple fill with neutral-white text.
- Secondary: transparent or neutral surface with a clear border.
- Destructive: accessible warning treatment, never yellow-only.
- All buttons require hover, focus, active, disabled, and loading states.

## Accessibility
Yellow is not suitable for small white text. Check contrast for every text/background pair. Do not communicate status through color alone. Provide visible focus indicators and keyboard support.

## Avoid
- Saturated unrelated palette additions.
- Large yellow backgrounds everywhere.
- Excessive gradients.
- Heavy glassmorphism.
- Unnecessary animation.
- Generic template aesthetics.
- Fake business claims.
